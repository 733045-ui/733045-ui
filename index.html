Description

Seals, also known as Pinnipeds, are mostly marine animals and one of the best animals according to everyone except biologists. These silly little creatures are super curious and like napping, their diet mostly containing squid, fish and other marine animals (some breeds even eating other seals). Seals range between 1 meter to 5 meters and 45 kg to 3,200

Cool Facts About Seals!

  • They can detect prey from up to 100 meters away using their whiskers!
  • There are 33 Types!
  • Seals can hold their breath for up to 2 hours!
